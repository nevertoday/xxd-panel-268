## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a tiny personal mark, not a full illustration and not a commercial logo. Compress the subject to the contour, pose and few flat shapes that still identify it. Keep that mark small inside a large quiet field; it may sit centered, off-center, against an edge or slightly cropped. Use soft lines, a little paper grain and imperfect handmade edges. Color is a short warm palette taken from the photograph, not a copy of every original hue. Typography is only a small editorial note or collection label. Reject a complete scene, cartoon rendering, template logos and decorative clutter.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
